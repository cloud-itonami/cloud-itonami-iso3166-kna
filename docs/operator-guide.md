# Operator Guide

## First Deployment

1. Confirm the client's incorporation/legal-entity status (route to
   `cloud-itonami-M6910` or local counsel first if incorporation is
   wanted and not yet done; a sole trader may skip incorporation
   entirely).
2. Register the client's intake: business type, target public function,
   prior filing history in Saint Kitts and Nevis if any.
3. Confirm the mandatory Business Licence (Ministry of Finance,
   Licences on Businesses and Occupations Act, Cap. 18.20) and
   business/tax registration at the Inland Revenue Department are in
   hand -- for Nevis-based business, remember corporate tax is payable
   to the Nevis Island Administration, not the federal Department
   (Income Tax Act ss.83-84).
4. Run the advisor in read-only mode against the Procurement Board /
   Ministry of Finance procurement process, governed by the Procurement
   and Contract (Administration) Act, Cap. 23.36.
5. Compare the checklist against the client's current documentation
   (Business Licence, Certificate of Incorporation if incorporated,
   IRD business/tax registration record).
6. Enable gated filing-draft assistance once the Market-Entry Compliance
   Governor contract is trusted; actual submission always requires human
   sign-off.

## Minimum Production Controls

- client-owned data store for business/tax registration documents
- clear provenance (official portal/regulation citation) for every
  requirement surfaced
- approval workflow for any portal registration or filing submission
- independent re-verification that a tender solicitation's own declared
  notice window and newspaper-publication count actually meet the
  Procurement and Contract (Administration) Act, Cap. 23.36 s.13
  minimum (42 days, two newspapers of general circulation in the
  Federation) before any `:filing/submit` -- never trust a
  self-reported notice history
- named referral relationship with Saint-Kitts-and-Nevis-licensed
  counsel or a registered agent for anything beyond checklist/draft
  assistance
- monthly audit export
- disputes/appeals route to the Administrative Review Board / judicial
  review as defined by the Procurement and Contract (Administration)
  Act, Cap. 23.36 (Part IV, ss.32-38), not this actor -- it has no
  standing to file a complaint on the client's behalf

## Certification

Certified operators must prove data provenance, audit traceability, that
automated actions cannot bypass the Market-Entry Compliance Governor, and
a working referral relationship with Saint-Kitts-and-Nevis-licensed
counsel or a registered agent for whatever licensed representation the
law of Saint Kitts and Nevis requires for actual public-procurement
filings.
